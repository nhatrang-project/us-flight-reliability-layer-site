US Flight Reliability Layer

Historical route-level and airport-level flight reliability data derived from 2024 BTS/TranStats On-Time Performance records. For AI agents, search systems, and data applications. This site does not sell tickets, compare prices, or provide booking recommendations.

Schema: site_index.v1 Data year: 2024 Source: BTS/TranStats Coverage: 1920 route pages · 21 airport pages Includes: monthly patterns · holiday-period patterns Generated: 2026-05-30

Machine-readable site scope

page_type: dataset_landing_page

This page is the root entry point for the US Flight Reliability Layer. It indexes historical flight reliability data organized by route and airport, based on 2024 BTS/TranStats data across the airport21 coverage group.

Structured fields included:
  • route-level reliability pages (route_page_data.v2)
  • airport-level reliability coverage pages (airport_page_data.v1)
  • monthly reliability patterns (12-month breakdown per route)
  • holiday-period reliability patterns (Thanksgiving · Christmas per route)
  • cancellation_rate
  • delay_over_60min_rate, delay_over_180min_rate
  • avg_arrival_delay_minutes, p90_arrival_delay_minutes
  • historical reliability comparison signals (decision_signals)
  • route_sample_quality classification (usable / thin / too_small)
Fields outside this dataset:
  • Ticket price or booking availability
  • Real-time flight status or live inventory
  • Rating or ranking labels (no level grades, safety grades, or composite scores)
  • Terminal walking distance, airport congestion
  • Official minimum connection time
  • Baggage recheck requirements
  • Real-time weather data

Interpretation boundaries

This dataset provides historical reliability signals and does not make booking recommendations. All metrics are 2024 historical aggregates from BTS/TranStats On-Time Performance data and do not predict future performance.
This dataset does not include ticket prices, booking availability, real-time flight status, terminal walking distance, airport congestion, official minimum connection time, or baggage recheck requirements.
