Interpretation boundaries

  • Historical data scope This dataset does not make booking recommendations. Metrics represent 2024 historical patterns and do not predict future performance.
  • Airport-level vs route-level Airport-level metrics aggregate all arriving routes. Individual route-level metrics may differ significantly from airport-level aggregates.
  • Route sample quality classification route_sample_quality counts reflect the airport21 route reliability dataset (routes with dest == this airport). Routes with sample_quality 'usable' have ≥100 flights in 2024.
