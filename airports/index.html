Interpretation boundaries

This index page and the linked airport pages provide 2024 historical arrival reliability signals based on BTS/TranStats On-Time Performance data. These pages do not make booking recommendations, do not represent real-time flight status, and do not rank airports as safer or less safe than others. Airport-level metrics aggregate all arriving routes — individual route metrics may differ. Metrics reflect historical patterns and do not predict future performance.
